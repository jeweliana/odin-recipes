Spaghetti

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 40 min · Servings: 4

Classic Spaghetti Bolognese

Classic Spaghetti Bolognese is a comforting Italian inspired pasta dish made with tender ground beef, rich tomato sauce, garlic, and aromatic herbs. Served over perfectly cooked spaghetti and topped with freshly grated Parmesan cheese, this hearty meal is perfect for a cozy family dinner.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 cup water
  • Grated Parmesan cheese, for serving
  • Fresh basil, for garnish(optional)

Steps

  1. Cook the Pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the spaghetti and cook according to the package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat oil: In a large skillet or saucepan, add oil and cook over medium heat.
  3. Simmer: Add the diced onion and cook for 3–4 minutes until softened. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for another 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Cook the Ground Beef: Add the ground beef and cook for 6–8 minutes, breaking it apart with a spoon, until fully browned. Drain excess grease if necessary.
  5. Sauce: Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute. Add the crushed tomatoes, water, oregano, basil, salt, and black pepper. Stir until combined. Reduce the heat to low and simmer uncovered for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens.Taste the sauce and adjust the seasoning if needed.
  6. Finish and Serve: Divide the spaghetti among serving plates and spoon the Bolognese sauce over the top. Garnish with grated Parmesan cheese and fresh basil before serving.
