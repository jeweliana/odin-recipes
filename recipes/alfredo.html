Alfredo

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 30 min · Servings: 4

Traditional Roman Alfredo

A pasta dish with white sauce known for its rich, buttery profile.

Different styles are found in italy and the united states.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound (454g) dry fettuccine pasta
  • 1 stick(8 tablespoons/115g) unsalted butter cut into cubes
  • 4 ounces (approx 1 cup) Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese, finely grated
  • 1 tablespoon salt (for pasta water)
  • Freshly cracked black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Prep the Bowls: Preheat your serving bowls in a warm oven or with hot water. This prevents the sauce from cooling to fast and congealing when served
  2. Boil the Pasta: Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Use a bit less water than usual to keep the starches concentrated. Add the salt and drop in the fettuccine. Cook until it is aldente.
  3. Prep the Toss Base: While the pasta cooks, add all the cubed butter and half of the grated cheese into a large, heat-safe mixing bowl.
  4. Save the Water: Just before draining, scoop out one cup of starchy pasta water and set it aside. Drain the pasta.
  5. Emulsify: Transfwe the steaning hot pasta directly into the bowl over the butter and cheese. Pour in half of the reserved pasta water. Toss and stir the pasta vigorously. The heat will melt the cheese abd butter, turning the water into a smooth sauce.
  6. Finish and Serve: Gradually add the remaining cheese and a bit more pasta water if the sauce looks dry. Keep tossing until it forms a creamy coating. Serve immediately in your warm bowls with fresh black pepper.
